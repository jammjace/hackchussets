05

Motion

Rhythm, not noise. Reduced motion swaps every movement for an opacity fade, stops loops and shows final values at once.

150ms
Hover. Buttons lift 2px and darken slightly; cards gain a 2px ink border.
300ms
UI changes. Toggles, tabs, badge swaps. Pops overshoot 6–8%.
600ms
Reveals. Fade + 12–16px rise, cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). Lists stagger 60ms.
900ms
Hero moments. The dish total counting up, the character settling in.

11

Copy tone

Warm, direct, slightly cheeky. Sentence case. Short sentences. British spelling. Talk about the dish, not the person.

Say
Say nah to hidden sodium.
Cook like you always do. We’ll count the salt.
“This dish is over the limit.” · “Most of it was the broth.” · “Not in your pantry yet. Add the label and we’ll count it next time.”
Don’t say
“You consumed too much sodium.” (about the person) · “Diagnosis: hypertension risk.” (clinical; we estimate, never diagnose) · “Unknown ingredient: 0 mg.” (never zero; flag it) · “Live sodium tracking.” (processing is never live) · Title Case Buttons · emoji.
