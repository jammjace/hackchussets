# Product

Sodium tracker web app for home cooks managing high blood pressure (Latino and Vietnamese American households first).

Flow: landing → pantry (editable, mocked receipt scan) → how to record → upload cooking video → processing → results.

Results show an interactive ingredient timeline: every seasoning added, its video time, detected quantity, sodium contribution and evidence photo. Clicking an item seeks the video to that moment. Total sodium for the dish is compared with the 2,300 mg daily limit.

Hackathon demo. Estimates only, not medical advice. English UI, British spelling.
